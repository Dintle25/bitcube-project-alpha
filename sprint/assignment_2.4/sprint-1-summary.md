## Executive Summary
Sprint 1 was successful overall. The team delivered the core booking functionality, allowing employees to book and cancel rooms. We achieved a velocity of 16 points and learned important lessons about estimation and task complexity.


## Metrics Snapshot
- Planned Points: 21  
- Completed Points: 13  
- Actual Velocity: 13

## Key Accomplishments
- Successfully implemented basic room booking with availability checks
- Added capacity filtering and booking cancellation
- No critical bugs found in core booking flow
- Strong team collaboration

## Challenges Faced
- Underestimated the complexity of equipment filtering
- Slow database queries delayed progress for two days
- Too many stories started at the same time

## Process Improvements Identified
- Break large stories into smaller tasks earlier
- Raise blockers immediately instead of waiting
- Be more conservative when selecting stories for the sprint

## Velocity and Burndown
- Planned Velocity: 21 points
- Actual Velocity: 16 points
- Burndown was steady in the first week but slowed down in Week 2 due to unexpected complexity in Story #5.

## Delivery Confidence for Next Sprint
- Medium
- We now have better understanding of the codebase and our real velocity. With improved task breakdown and earlier blocker raising, we expect better performance in Sprint 2.
