# Sprint 1 — Personal Reflection

## Key Learning
The most important lesson I learned is that work takes longer than expected, especially when integrating frontend and backend. Even simple-looking stories can have hidden complexities.

## Assumptions That Failed
My biggest wrong assumption was that estimation would be accurate in the first sprint. I thought we could complete 21 points easily, but we only finished 16. I also assumed everyone would finish their tasks without needing much help.

## Behavioural Change
In Sprint 2, I will raise blockers on the same day instead of trying to solve them alone first.

## Professional Growth
I need to strengthen my ability to break down stories into smaller, manageable tasks and improve my estimation skills. I also want to get better at facilitating retrospectives and helping the team stay focused.
